## Key Principles & Frameworks

### 1. Kallaway's Viral Hook Stack
Derived from short-form strategist Kallaway (1B+ views, 1M+ followers):
- **Eliminate Delay:** Hook context lands in under 2 seconds.
- **Eliminate Confusion:** Crisp, conversational language; zero high-friction jargon.
- **Eliminate Irrelevance:** Immediate personal stake or universal emotional trigger.
- **Eliminate Disinterest:** Open a tension-filled curiosity loop or challenge a widely held belief.
- **3-Layer Hook:** Visual Hook + Verbal Hook + Text Hook firing synchronously.

### 2. The 10 Psychological Thumbnail & Header Banner Archetypes
1. **The Curiosity Gap:** Open an incomplete thought loop.
2. **Negativity & Loss Aversion:** Urgent warnings, mistakes to stop immediately.
3. **Before vs. After:** Stark visual transformation.
4. **Extreme Contrast ("Versus"):** Pitting two titans or extremes together.
5. **Pattern-Interrupt Number:** Specific, odd numbers breaking the feed trance.
6. **The Burning Question:** Tapping into hot ongoing debates.
7. **Contrarian Authority:** Direct, counter-intuitive bold declarations.
8. **Authentic Emotion + Gaze Direction:** High-intensity real human connection.
9. **The Unexpected Quote:** Standout provocative soundbites from dialogue.
10. **The Mid-Action Freeze Frame:** High-stakes suspense right before the impact.

## How to Use

When you provide a topic, draft script, or video transcript:
1. The skill identifies the **Core Promise** and emotional payoff.
2. Formulates **3 distinct angles** (Curiosity Loop, Warning/Mistake, Contrarian/Versus).
3. Produces paired packages in **Tanglish**, **Tamil**, and **English**:
   - **Title:** Under 60 characters (prevents truncation on mobile).
   - **Banner / Thumbnail Text:** Strictly 2 to 4 punchy words.
   - **5-Second Opening Hook:** 1 to 2 spoken sentences crafted for maximum first-3-seconds retention.
