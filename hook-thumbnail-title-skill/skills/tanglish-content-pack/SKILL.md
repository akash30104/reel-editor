# Tanglish Content Pack & Viral Hook Generator

Generate paired **Titles**, **Thumbnail / Header Banner Text**, and **5-Second Opening Hooks** across **Tanglish**, **Tamil**, and **English** for high-retention short-form Reels and long-form podcasts.

Integrates **Kallaway's Short-Form Viral Hook Engine** (3-layer hook stack, 4 fatal hook mistakes) with **10 psychological thumbnail & packaging archetypes**.

---

## Kallaway's 4 Fatal Hook Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Delay (TTV - Time to Value Too High):** Context must be delivered within the first 1 to 2 seconds. Never start with greetings, self-introductions, slow establishing shots, or "In today's video we're going to talk about...".
2. **Confusion (Comprehension Loss):** Avoid convoluted words or stiff textbook jargon. If a viewer has to think twice to understand what you just said, their thumb swipes away.
3. **Irrelevance:** Fail to identify who the video is for or what universal emotion is at stake. Make the topic feel personally consequential to the viewer right away.
4. **Disinterest (Zero Tension / No Stakes):** Without an open loop, contrarian claim, or burning question, there is no psychological friction compelling the viewer to stay.

---

## Kallaway's 3-Layer Hook Architecture

Every winning video hook must fire all 3 layers simultaneously in the first 2 seconds:
- **Layer 1: Visual Hook:** Dynamic camera punch-in, high-contrast on-screen movement, eye contact, or split-screen contrast.
- **Layer 2: Verbal Hook:** 1 to 2 spoken sentences that trigger loss aversion, challenge a belief, or pose an unresolved dilemma.
- **Layer 3: Text Hook (On-Screen Banner / Thumbnail Text):** 2 to 4 bold words in high-contrast color (Signature Orange `#F38300` / `#EA8207`) that supply immediate context and an incomplete curiosity loop.

## The 10 Psychological Hook & Thumbnail Archetypes

1. **The Curiosity Gap (The Incomplete Thought):** Open a mental loop without closing it. Viewers click/watch to resolve the cognitive itch.
   *Example:* `"I Regret Reading This..."`, `"The Scene They Censored"`.
2. **Negativity & Loss Aversion:** Humans fear making mistakes or being deceived more than they seek gains.
   *Example:* `"Big Mistake,"` `"Stop Doing This,"` `"99% Wasting Time"`.
3. **Before vs. After Transformation:** Show two starkly different states side-by-side.
   *Example:* `"Day 1 vs Day 30,"` `"Ordinary Hunter vs Brother of God"`.
4. **Extreme Contrast ("Versus"):** Pitting two titans or extremes against each other creates instant tension.
   *Example:* `"Kambar vs Valmiki,"` `"10-Headed King vs Barefoot Hermit"`.
5. **The Pattern-Interrupt Number:** Specific, odd numbers break the visual scroll trance.
   *Example:* `"14 Years,"` `"5th Brother,"` `"100% Fail Rate"`.
6. **The Burning Question:** Tap into a debate the viewer is already having in their head or on social media.
   *Example:* `"Is It Real?",` `"Did Kambar Change The Story?",` `"Why Forgive A Traitor?"`.
7. **Contrarian Authority:** Challenge a widely accepted belief with confident counter-logic.
   *Example:* `"Rama Was Wrong?",` `"Ravana Wasn't The Real Villain"`.
8. **Authentic Emotion + Gaze Direction:** Real, unforced emotional expression (disbelief, fierce conviction, tears) looking right down the lens or guiding the gaze.
9. **The Unexpected Quote (Sound Bite):** A punchy, controversial line extracted directly from dialogue.
   *Example:* `"Unnodu Naanga 5 Aanaom!",` `"Padippai Vida Dharmam Mukkiyam"`.
10. **The Mid-Action Freeze Frame:** High-stakes moment right before the reveal or impact.

## Core Packaging Rules

1. **Title and Thumbnail Must Complement, Never Repeat:**
   - The **Title** states the core topic or high-stakes premise.
   - The **Thumbnail / Header Banner** delivers the curiosity gap, punchline, or emotional trigger in 2 to 4 words.
2. **Banner / Thumbnail Text Limit:** Strictly 2 to 4 short words so it remains instantly readable on small mobile screens.
3. **Natural Conversational Phrasing:**
   - **Tanglish:** Spoken Tamil Nadu street / podcast conversational style in English script (e.g., `"Kambar Pannadha Valmiki Pannala!"`, `"Ippadi Oru Twist-a?"`).
   - **Tamil:** Natural spoken Tamil script without archaic or bookish grammar (`"வால்மீகி சொன்ன உண்மை இதுதான்!"`).
   - **English:** High-velocity, mobile-first punchy copy (`"The Forbidden Secret"`).
4. **Zero Clickbait Deception:** High curiosity, but 100% truthful to what the speakers actually discuss in the video.
