# Reference 5 Style (Ref5) Master Production Skill

Use this skill whenever editing videos in the **Ref5 / Reference 5** aesthetic.

## Production Specification:
1. **Format**: 9:16 Vertical (`1080x1920 @ 30fps`).
2. **Pacing**: Fast-paced conversational cuts (<3–4s static shot duration).
3. **Cold Open Front Hook**: Cut the most compelling punchline/statement to the very beginning (0.0s).
4. **B-Roll Density**: At least 70% contextual, narrative-enhancing B-roll.
5. **Attention Reset**: Exactly 1-second solid `#0A0A0A` Jet Black pause right before the punchline/pivot.
6. **Strict 3-Color Palette**:
   - Bright Yellow: `#FFF200` (`RGB(255, 242, 0)`)
   - Pure White: `#FFFFFF` (`RGB(255, 255, 255)`)
   - Jet Black: `#0A0A0A` (`RGB(10, 10, 10)`)
   - No other accent colors permitted.
7. **Two-Tier Typography**:
   - **Tier A (Kinetic Titles)**: High-contrast Serif (`Playfair Display` / `Georgia`) + Geometric Sans (`Montserrat` / `Inter`). Highlight boxes in `#FFF200` with `#0A0A0A` text.
   - **Tier B (Conversational Subs)**: Sans-Serif Bold (`Inter` / `Roboto`) in `#FFFFFF` on tight `#0A0A0A` boxes.
8. **Visual Interrupts**:
   - Desaturate to B&W during friction/questions.
   - 3D stickers & icons popping on key nouns.
9. **Audio Engineering**:
   - Dialogue normalized to `-14.0 LUFS` (`-1.0 dBTP`).
   - Ducked background music at `-23 dB`.
   - SFX: whooshes on camera transitions, sub-bass hits on punchlines, pops on floating stickers.
10. **Loop Ending**: Aspirational B-roll with 3-line kinetic title followed by an abrupt, seamless loop cut.
