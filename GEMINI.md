# Reference 5 Style (Ref5) Rules & Guidelines

When the user requests to edit in the "ref5", "reference 5", or "ref 5" style, strictly enforce the following production standards:

## 1. FORMAT & PACING
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 Vertical (`1080x1920`, 30fps or 60fps).
- **Pacing**: Fast, conversational, and retention-focused. Never hold a static talking-head shot for more than 3–4 seconds without a visual pattern interrupt (angle cut, scale punch, B-roll clip, floating PNG sticker, or kinetic text beat).
- **The Front Hook**: Always find the most viral, counter-intuitive, or provocative punchline from the raw footage, cut it, and place it at `0.0s` (the cold open hook).
- **70% Related B-Roll Rule**: At least 70% of the timeline must be covered with highly contextual, relatable B-roll (reaction memes, analogies, aspirational footage, lifestyle, science/brain imagery). Never use generic or unrelated filler.
- **Attention Reset Pause**: Include a brief 1-second solid Jet Black screen pause (`#0A0A0A`) right before a major pivot or punchline to reset viewer dopamine and attention.

## 2. STRICT 3-COLOR FONT PALETTE (NO OTHER COLORS)
Use ONLY the following three colors across all text, boxes, shadows, and highlights. No red, green, blue, or other accent colors:
- **Bright Yellow (`#FFF200` / `RGB(255, 242, 0)`)**: Hero words, key takeaways, punchlines, and highlight boxes.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF` / `RGB(255, 255, 255)`)**: Setup words, supporting text, and standard dialogue subtitles.
- **Jet Black (`#0A0A0A` / `RGB(10, 10, 10)`)**: Subtitle background boxes, drop shadows, and high-contrast warning/emphasis text (always placed inside a Bright Yellow highlight box for maximum readability).

## 3. TWO-TIER TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM
Alternate between two caption styles so the video is readable and engaging even on mute:

### A. Stylized Kinetic Titles (Hooks, Questions & Key Punchlines)
- **Font Pairing**:
  - **Hero Words**: High-contrast Serif (`Playfair Display` or `Georgia`). Use *Italic* for dynamic/punchy words and Regular ALL-CAPS for authoritative words. Large (~10%–18% of screen height), centered or upper-mid frame.
  - **Setup Words**: Clean geometric Sans-Serif (`Montserrat`, `Poppins`, or `Inter`). Use Light/Regular weight with wide letter-spacing for setup phrases, and Extra-Bold ALL-CAPS for hard stops. Medium (~4%–6% of screen height).
- **Styling**:
  - Standard emphasis: White Sans-Serif setup + Bright Yellow Serif hero word, with a soft Jet Black drop shadow behind floating text.
  - Hard stops / Warnings / Negatives: Jet Black Extra-Bold Sans-Serif or Serif Italic placed inside a solid Bright Yellow rectangular highlight box (`#FFF200`).
  - Dynamic word swaps: Anchor top/bottom setup words while swapping middle hero words on the dialogue beat.

### B. Standard Conversational Subtitles (Fast Dialogue)
- **Font**: Clean Sans-Serif Bold/Semi-Bold (`Roboto`, `Inter`, or `Arial Bold`).
- **Size**: Uniform (~2.5%–3% of screen height), locked to the lower-third center (safe zone above platform UI).
- **Styling**: Pure White text (`#FFFFFF`) inside a solid Jet Black rectangular background box (`#0A0A0A`) with tight padding. (Optionally invert to Jet Black text on a Bright Yellow box for a punchline beat).

## 4. VISUAL EFFECTS & PATTERN INTERRUPTS
- **Selective Color / B&W Beat**: During an intense question or friction point, desaturate the footage to Black & White with a subtle vintage film grain/dust overlay while keeping Bright Yellow highlight boxes and Jet Black/White typography crisp on top.
- **Floating Stickers & PNGs**: Pop in 3D icons, cutouts, or meme stickers around the speaker when mentioning key nouns, platforms, chemicals, or analogies.
- **Audio & SFX**:
  - Master dialogue normalized to `-14.0 LUFS` (`-1.0 dBTP`).
  - Ducked background music (`-22 dB` to `-24 dB`).
  - Tactile sound effects on cuts and visual transitions: whoosh on cuts, sub-bass hit/impact on punchlines, pop SFX on stickers.
- **Ending**: Close on an aspirational/high-energy B-roll shot paired with a large kinetic title (`White Sans-Serif` + `Huge Yellow Serif` + `White Serif All-Caps`), followed by an abrupt, tight cut for an infinite loop.
